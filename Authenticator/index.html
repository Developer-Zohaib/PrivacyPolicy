This Privacy Policy explains how Zohaib Afzal ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and discloses information about you when you use our iOS application, Authenticator App, and related services (collectively, the "Services").

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. If we make changes, we will notify you by revising the "Last Updated" date at the top of this policy. We encourage you to review this Privacy Policy periodically to stay informed about our data protection practices.

Security & Privacy First: Authenticator App is designed to generate secure Two-Factor Authentication (2FA) tokens. Your secret keys and generated OTP codes are stored securely on your device (or encrypted in your personal iCloud account) and are never accessible by us.

1. Collection of Information

Information You Provide to Us

We collect information you directly provide to us, which may include:

  • Account & Contact Info: Email address or support inquiry details when you contact us for customer service or feedback.
  • Purchase & Subscription Info: Transaction details processed via Apple App Store in-app purchases (e.g., subscription tier, activation date). We do not collect or store full credit card details.

Authenticator Credentials Data

When you scan QR codes or manually add 2FA account credentials (secret keys, account names, issuer details), this information is strictly processed locally on your device or synced securely via your personal Apple iCloud Account. We do not have access to your secret keys or two-factor tokens.

Information Collected Automatically

When you access or use our App, we may automatically collect certain non-sensitive usage information, including:

  • Usage Data: Basic analytics regarding features accessed, crash logs, and app stability performance.
  • Device Information: Mobile hardware model, iOS version, unique device identifiers set by your operating system, preferred language, and regional settings.
  • Transactional Data: Purchase confirmation receipts provided by Apple to unlock premium features.

2. Use of Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide, maintain, and optimize the functionality of Authenticator App.
  • Process and verify in-app purchases and subscription entitlements.
  • Respond to your customer support requests, questions, or technical inquiries.
  • Monitor and analyze usage trends, fix bugs, and improve overall app reliability.
  • Protect against fraudulent transactions, unauthorized access, and illegal activities.
  • Comply with applicable legal and financial obligations.

3. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

  • Service Providers: With third-party service providers who assist us with cloud backend infrastructure, crash reporting, and analytics tools under strict confidentiality obligations.
  • Apple App Store Ecosystem: Transactions and subscriptions are handled directly through Apple’s secure App Store infrastructure.
  • Legal Compliance: In response to legal obligations, court orders, or requests from legal authorities if required by law.
  • Protection of Rights: If necessary to protect the rights, safety, property, and integrity of the App, its users, or the developer.

4. Analytics and Third-Party Services

We may integrate third-party privacy-focused analytics tools (e.g., Apple App Analytics or Firebase Crashlytics) to help us understand app performance and stability. These services may collect device identifiers, performance metrics, and OS specifications. These third parties do not receive any access to your 2FA credentials or secret keys.
